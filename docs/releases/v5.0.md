# Suno Workbench v5

Suno Workbench v5 builds on v4 with a major rewrite of the offline Styles generator for bass, club, and rap music, more reliable hybrid-genre composition, and a fully searchable Guide. The new native generation path focuses on preserving the actual brief—its genre, tempo, mood, instruments, and vocal direction—while producing more coherent, genre-specific Styles prompts.

The core privacy model is unchanged: **everything runs in your browser.** There is no application server, LLM, or external API call, and your prompts and lyrics are not sent away for processing. The self-contained offline HTML edition remains available.

## Highlights

- Rebuilt offline Styles generation for bass, club, and rap around **29 dedicated native genre banks**, each with its own tempo range, drum signatures, and bass design.
- Set the native generator's Styles target to **750–900 characters**, with a **1,000-character hard cap**.
- Added a hard **genre-bleed gate** that rejects unrequested acoustic, band, orchestral, and lo-fi language in bass, club, and rap prompts.
- Improved two- and three-genre blends with a stable primary genre, explicit supporting roles, reconciled moods, and fewer repeated concepts.
- Separated **riddim from wobble** and expanded native support for drill, phonk, bass, club, and modern rap styles.
- Added rap-aware Lyrics Studio delivery tags, bracketed ad-libs, additional genre options, and arrangements without EDM Drop sections.
- Replaced the Guide with a chapter-based, locally searchable manual, including mobile navigation and offline access.
- Cleaned up compiler phrasing, fixed an ineffective phonetics rule, and expanded project and contributor documentation.
- Passed **371 automated tests**, TypeScript checking, and the production/offline build.

## Offline Styles Generator: Native Bass, Club, and Rap

The new native generator is built around musical behavior instead of reusing one general phrase pipeline for every style.

- **Brief fidelity:** Keep the named genre, BPM, mood, instruments, vocal descriptors, and vocal effects in the resulting production direction.
- **Prompt budget:** Aim for 750–900 Styles characters, with a 1,000-character hard limit for the native route.
- **Native sound design:** Use a genre's own drum language, low-end movement, rhythmic feel, and production signatures.
- **Multi-genre focus:** Keep two- and three-genre blends within the requested bass/club/rap sound world rather than importing unrelated musical vocabulary.

### 29 Dedicated Native Genre Banks

Each bank has its own tempo range and signature drum and bass vocabulary.

- **Dubstep family (9):** dubstep, brostep, deep dubstep, UKG dubstep, wobble, riddim, tearout, deathstep, and glitch bass.
- **Drum and bass (2):** drum & bass and drumstep.
- **Club (6):** bass house, UK garage, future house, afro tech house, moombahton, and baile funk.
- **Bass EDM (3):** bass EDM, future bass, and future beats.
- **Phonk (3):** phonk, drift phonk, and Brazilian phonk.
- **Trap and rap (6):** EDM trap, trap rap, modern rap, autotuned sung-rap, UK drill, and NY drill.

**Riddim and wobble are now distinct native styles**, rather than variations of one interchangeable bass profile. The newer drill and phonk selections also receive their own production-specific wording.

## Hard Genre-Bleed Protection

A new validation gate protects the intended musical identity of bass, club, and rap prompts.

- **noGenreBleed:** Rejects unrequested acoustic, band, orchestral, or lo-fi language.
- **non_edm_bleed:** Marks the corresponding contradiction when the generated Styles wording violates that constraint.
- **Explicit requests still matter:** The gate targets unrequested terminology rather than rewriting a deliberate creative brief.
- **Phonk exception:** Phonk can retain its characteristic cassette texture and lo-fi grit.

This is a hard validation rule, not merely a recommendation to the generator.

## Better Hybrid-Genre Composition

Hybrid briefs now follow a clearer production hierarchy instead of assembling competing genre descriptions.

- **First-named genre stays primary:** Genre names are de-duplicated by hierarchy, and the original lead genre remains stable.
- **At most two supporting genres:** Each supporting style receives a defined role in the plan.
- **Reconciled moods:** Related moods are combined rather than repeated in conflicting sentences.
- **Less duplicated production language:** Concepts such as 808 bass, vocal chops, and mix priorities are expressed once where possible.
- **One coherent mix direction:** The renderer avoids repeating the same master mix line.
- **Cleaner Fix output:** The older compiler no longer treats its own filler and mismatched instrumentation lists as authoritative source instructions.
- **Wording cleanup:** Awkward grammar was improved and the previously used “gunshot” wording was removed.

## Genre-Aware Phrase Selection

The compiler's phrase selection now respects the relationship between the primary genre and the supporting styles.

- The **primary genre owns the overall plan**.
- Supporting genres contribute phrases only to the sections their assigned roles allow.
- Style-tagged expressions are used only when compatible with the named native genre.
- Unrequested G-funk, vinyl crackle, and ceremonial percussion no longer intrude into a trap + UKG dubstep blend.
- Fix mode removes compiler-written lists when they contradict the requested genre combination.

The goal is a Styles paragraph that describes one believable production, not a collection of loosely related genre phrases.

## Rap-Aware Lyrics Studio

Lyrics Studio now better separates rap delivery and song structure from EDM drop arrangement conventions.

- **Bracketed section and delivery labels:** for example, **[Verse 1 | autotuned sung-rap, melodic]** and **[Hook | chant]**.
- **Bracketed ad-libs:** ad-lib directions remain identifiable as performance guidance.
- **No Drop sections in rap lyrics:** rap arrangements use suitable song sections instead.
- **Expanded genre options:** additional bass, trap, drill, phonk, and rap choices are available within Lyrics Studio.
- **Named vocal details preserved:** vocal effects, delivery traits, and descriptions from the brief remain part of generation intent.

American-English phonetic support remains available in Lyrics Studio.

## New Searchable, Chapter-Based Guide

The Guide has been rebuilt from a **157-page source manuscript** into a navigable manual with **29 chapters plus three new front-matter chapters**.

- **Local-only full-text search:** find relevant passages without sending queries to a service.
- **Chapter navigation and deep links:** move directly to the material you need.
- **Mobile chapter selector:** a more usable navigation experience on smaller screens.
- **Light and dark layouts:** the Guide follows the Workbench theme.
- **Offline inclusion:** the searchable Guide is bundled into the self-contained HTML edition.

This makes the production documentation available alongside the tools, even without an internet connection.

## Phonetics, Documentation, and CI

A smaller set of fixes and maintenance changes improves reliability and contribution workflows.

- **Phonetics regression fix:** removed a no-op phonetic substitution flagged by static analysis and added a regression test.
- **README restructure:** updated the repository's introduction and project documentation.
- **Contributor guidance:** added **CONTRIBUTING.md**.
- **README Score automation:** added a documentation-scoring GitHub Actions workflow directly to the main branch.

These documentation and CI changes do not introduce a new runtime service or change where your prompts are processed.

## Behavior Changes and Compatibility

The new generator is intentionally scoped; not every compiler path was replaced.

- **Native route:** bass, club, and rap briefs—including the new drill and phonk options—use the dedicated native phrase and production banks.
- **Riddim:** routes separately from wobble.
- **Legacy route:** Draft mode, Rewrite mode, and genres not claimed by the native generator continue through the previous pipeline.

The **750–900-character target applies to the new native route**. Legacy generation paths retain their existing behavior.

## Privacy and Offline Architecture

Suno Workbench continues to run entirely client-side:

- No application backend or server-side prompt processing.
- No LLM or AI API call.
- No account or API key required.
- Prompts, lyrics, and generated content remain in the browser.
- The full application and searchable Guide remain available in a self-contained offline HTML edition.
