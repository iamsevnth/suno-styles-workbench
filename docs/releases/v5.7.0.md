# Suno Workbench v5.7.0 — Visible, Permission-Based AI

In v5.5.0 the AI option was a checkbox that appeared only in browsers with Chrome's built-in AI, and nothing said whether AI was in use. v5.7.0 makes AI use visible and asks permission at each step. **Generation is unchanged:** the engine writes every prompt without AI.

## Changed

- **AI Assist** (brief panel, Create mode) is always shown, with its status: **AI Off**, **AI On** or **Not Available**.
  - **Not Available** explains why: on-device AI needs desktop Chrome with Chrome's built-in AI (Gemini Nano) on a computer that can run it. Safari, Firefox and phones don't have it. A link goes to Chrome's requirements.
- **Permission before AI turns on.** The **Use on-device AI** switch opens a dialog that says exactly what happens:
  - AI runs only when you click **Reword with AI**, one result at a time, and never by itself.
  - It changes only wording; the engine still builds every prompt.
  - AI wording is always labeled.
  - The model runs in Chrome on your computer.

  **Keep AI Off** is the default button.
- **Permission before the model download.** If Chrome doesn't have its model yet, the first **Reword with AI** asks before Chrome downloads it from Google.
- **Labels everywhere:**
  - Every result shows **Workbench engine · no AI**.
  - The results header shows **No AI Used**, or **AI On · runs only when you click**.
  - AI wording sits in a violet **AI** section, labeled **AI wording · passed the Workbench checks**.
  - While the model works, the section says **AI is rewording this prompt on your device…**, and **Stop AI** cancels it.
- Turning AI off removes every AI control at once.
- The Privacy Policy describes the new flow.

## Validation

- 517 unit tests, TypeScript check and production build.
- 26 browser tests in desktop and mobile Chromium. New ones cover:
  - **Not Available:** the status, the disabled switch, the explanation, and the "No AI Used" and engine labels.
  - **Permission:** declining keeps AI off; agreeing turns it on.
  - **On demand:** no AI call happens until Reword with AI is clicked.
  - **Labels and checks:** AI wording is labeled and checked, and rule-breaking wording is discarded.
  - **Download:** Chrome asks before downloading its model, and cancelling makes no call.
  - **Off:** turning AI off removes every AI control.

  These tests include axe WCAG 2.1 A/AA scans.
- The browser tests use a stand-in for Chrome's API, because the test machines can't run the real model.
