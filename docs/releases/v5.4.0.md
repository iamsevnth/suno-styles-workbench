# Suno Workbench v5.4.0 — Brief Check, Role Owners and Named Variation Changes

v5.4.0 is the deterministic part of the prompt-engine upgrade built from the prompt-engine research of 11/23/2026 ([rules](../research/prompt-engine-rules-2026-11-23.json), [research notes](../research/advanced-prompt-engineering-research-2026-11-23.md)). **Styles generation is unchanged**: the same brief, seed and settings produce the same prompts as v5.3.2. Everything still runs in the browser with no AI or network calls.

## Added

- **Brief Check** (Create mode, under the brief). Updates as you type and never generates anything. Each note names what the brief leaves ambiguous and suggests a rewrite. Rule ids follow the research file:
  - `TOO_MANY_EQUAL_GENRES`: three or more genres with no roles. After a generation of the same brief, the note names the roles the engine chose.
  - `TEMPO_CONVENTION_CONFLICT`: a requested BPM far from a genre's usual range (one note, all genres listed).
  - `TWO_PRIMARY_KICK_SYSTEMS`: 2-step and four-on-the-floor at once, with no sections given.
  - `MASSIVE_FULL_RANGE_BASS_STACK`: four or more competing low-end sources (808, sub, Reese, wobble, growl/FM, neuro, acid, donk).
  - `CONSTANT_MAX_HAT_DENSITY`: continuous 32nd-note hats, or 32nds in a deep or sparse groove.
  - `ELECTRONIC_HATS_EXCLUDED`: electronic hats or hat rolls requested while hats are excluded.
  - `UNDEFINED_HOOK`: no hook, lead, riff, chop or vocal named (electronic and bass-music briefs only; other genres bring a lead from their catalog).
  - `UNSPECIFIED_SECOND_DROP`: a second drop described only as bigger or heavier.

  Words after "no", "without", "exclude" or "avoid" never trigger the positive checks. Checks read the brief clause by clause: kick patterns, bass sources or 32nd-note hats tied to their own section (or a call-and-answer role) are not flagged, and following a note's own suggestion clears it. Notes are heuristics, not Suno rules, and never block generation.
- **Role owners** in each result's Sonic Plan: which genre owns the groove, the low end, the harmony and the hook, with the clause that carries each, and which genres only add color. Replaces the Rhythm Donor and Bass Donor cells.
- **Named variation changes:** from the second result on, the card says where it differs musically from the first (rhythm, bass, sound design, arrangement, vocals). The existing rule that rejects and regenerates results differing in fewer than two of these axes (Precision and Exploration; A/B Lab changes only the bass articulation by design) is unchanged and now visible. The Sonic Plan's "Variation Recipe" no longer shows internal seed and recipe ids.

## Not checked (and why)

The research also lists rules for Suno's create screen: audio influence, voice ids, mashup and reference counts, simple vs. custom mode, rights for source audio, and credit-spending actions. The Workbench writes text for the Styles field and never touches those settings, so it does not check them. Styles length is already a hard gate (800–1,000 characters).
