# Suno Workbench v5.7.1 — AI Permission Fixes

Fixes four problems in v5.7.0's AI permission flow, found in review after release. **Generation is unchanged.**

## Fixed

- **No carried-over AI setting.** v5.5 and v5.6 saved "AI on" from a plain checkbox. v5.7.0 restored that setting, so AI came back on without the new permission dialog. v5.7.1 ignores and removes the old setting, so AI starts off and asks first. The new setting is stored only after you agree in the dialog.
- **One download question.** After you agree to Chrome's model download on one result, other results, including later generations in the same visit, don't ask again, and a question already open on another result closes.
- **Keyboard focus.** Tab and Shift+Tab stay inside the permission dialog until you choose. Escape or **Keep AI Off** returns focus to the AI switch.
- **AI can always be turned off.** AI Assist is shown in Fix Existing Prompt mode too. Before, it was hidden there while results still had AI controls.

## Validation

- 517 unit tests, TypeScript check and production build.
- 30 browser tests in desktop and mobile Chromium. New ones cover:
  - an old "on" setting starting as **AI Off** and being removed;
  - Tab and Shift+Tab staying in the dialog, and focus returning to the switch;
  - a second result rewording without another download question;
  - turning AI off in Fix mode.
