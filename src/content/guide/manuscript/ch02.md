# Quick Start & Interface Anatomy

## 2.1 The 60-Second End-to-End Producer Workflow
Whether you are generating a track from a blank slate or translating a complex hybrid brief into
Suno v6, the core Suno Workbench loop takes less than 60 seconds from concept to rendered
audio:

     Step 1 — Enter or Sample a Brief: Leave the Creative Brief box empty for a pure weighted
      Master Library draw, click 'Surprise Brief' to load one of 24 curated hybrid seeds, click one of
      the 3 rotating Quick-Suggestion Pills, or type your own genres, moods, instruments, and
      tempo.
     Step 2 — Set Compiler Locks & v6 Context: Optionally set BPM Lock (60–200), Vocal Override
      (Auto, Female, Male, Duet, Chops, Instrumental), or Blend Mode (Auto, Tame, Weird,
      Unhinged), and select your target Suno v6 Model and Variety stop in the Suno v6 Bar.
     Step 3 — Compile 3 Verified Variants: Click 'Compile 3 Styles Prompts' (or press Cmd/Ctrl +
      Enter). In under 5 milliseconds, the compiler outputs 3 distinct 925–975 character prompts,
      each with a custom 3-word Title, character gauge, 6-gate badge strip, provenance breakdown,
      and v6 slider prescription.
     Step 4 — Paste into Suno v6 Custom Mode: Click 'Copy Styles' on your preferred variant card
      and paste directly into Suno's Custom Mode Styles field. Keep Suno's Variety slider at 0 (Off),
      Personalize (My Taste) Off, and set Style Influence and Weirdness to the card's recommended
      percentages.
     Step 5 — Format Lyrics & Phonetics: If your track includes vocals, switch to the Lyrics Studio
      or retired production playbooks tab to structure section meta-tags ([Intro], [Verse], [Pre-Chorus], [Chorus],
      [Drop], [Outro]) and run the American Phonetics Converter on problem words before clicking
      Create in Suno.

## 2.3 Styles Compiler Workspace: Input Controls & Pills
Every input element in the Styles Compiler workspace is wired directly to src/lib/suno/parse.ts,
src/lib/suno/suggestions.ts, and src/lib/suno/v6.ts:

 Control Name                  Exact Behavior & Underlying Source Constant

 Creative Brief Textarea       Accepts 0 to 500+ characters of freeform producer notes, genre names,
                               mood adjectives, instruments, BPM numbers, or vocal cues. Supports
                               Cmd/Ctrl + Enter to trigger compilation immediately.

 Compile 3 Styles Prompts      Primary action button. Generates a fresh 32-bit seed (unless a manual
                               Seed number is locked) and runs the 7-stage deterministic pipeline to
                               output 3 verified variants.

 Surprise Brief Button         Selects a random high-contrast brief from SURPRISE_BRIEF (24 curated
                               multi-genre briefs in suggestions.ts) and immediately compiles 3 variants.

 3 Quick-Suggestion Pills      Displays 3 clickable brief pills (<= 78 characters each, SUGGESTION_MAX =
                               78, SUGGESTION_COUNT = 3) drawn from suggestionPool() (~196 curated
                               combinations across Master Library, Atlas collisions, and Surprise briefs).

 Refresh Suggestions (↻)       Re-rolls the 3 Quick-Suggestion Pills using a fresh PRNG draw without
                               clearing your current Creative Brief textarea.

 Clear Brief Button            Empties the Creative Brief textarea and resets transient input warnings
                               while preserving your History drawer.

## 2.5 Anatomy of a Compiled Variant Output Card
Each compilation produces three side-by-side (or vertically stacked) Variant Cards (#1, #2, and
#3). Every element on a Variant Card provides actionable engineering telemetry:





    Card Component            Engineering Function & Producer Utility

    Variant Role Badge        Top-left badge indicating the structural rotation: Variant #1 (Primary
                              Spine Lead), Variant #2 (Secondary Genre Lead), or Variant #3 (Hybrid
                              / Contrast Lead).

    Legacy track-title field (retired)     A deterministic 3-word Title Case track name (e.g., 'Obsidian Sub
                              Ritual' or 'Velvet Garage Horizon') generated by formatTitle() in
                              render.ts with a 1-click Copy Title button.

    Legacy hexadecimal prompt ID (retired)      Unique identifier (e.g., #a4f91c02) generated by randomPromptId() in
                              v6.ts for tracking takes across exported text sheets and DAW stems.

    Character Budget          Live counter and visual progress bar showing the exact character
    Gauge                     count (e.g., 954 / 975 chars) within the 925–975 green target window.

    7-Sentence Compiled       The copy-ready Styles prompt block containing zero line breaks, zero
    Prose                     artist names, zero negations, and exact punctuation engineered for
                              Suno's Styles box.

    6-Gate Validation Strip   Green checkmarks confirming pass status across all 6 hard gates:
                              Chars (925–975), BPM (Integer), Clean Refs (No Artist Leaks), Positive
                              Only, Styles Only, and Distinctness.

    Attribute Provenance      Color-coded breakdown of every sonic axis showing [Your Input] vs.
    Pills                     [Locked] vs. [Compiler-Inferred] attributes.

    Suno v6 Slider            Card-specific recommendation for Style Influence %, Weirdness %,
    Prescription              Variety (0/Off), and Vocal Gender/Weight alignment.

    Action Footer Buttons     'Copy Styles' (copies raw prompt), 'Copy Card + Metadata' (copies
                              formatted block with title, ID, BPM, and v6 notes), and 'Send to Skills
                              Studio'.
