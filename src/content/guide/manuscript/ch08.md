structured SonicPlan into natural-language studio prose. Crucially, the order of sentences is not
arbitrary: Suno's text encoder applies slightly stronger positional weighting to tokens appearing
in the first 250 characters and the final 150 characters of the Styles block. Suno Workbench

## 8.2 The 6-Pass Expansion & 5-Pass Compression Cascades
After assembling the initial 7 sentences, renderStyles() measures the exact string length against
MIN_CHARS (925), MAX_CHARS (975), and TARGET_CHARS (952). Rather than crudely chopping
words off the end (which would create broken grammar) or repeating phrases (which causes
token loops), render.ts runs a multi-pass surgical adjustment engine:

   The 6-Pass Expansion Cascade (When < 925 Chars): If length < 925: (Pass 1) Appends a
    family-matched Sonic DNA clause from lexicon-dna.ts; (Pass 2) Appends a master bus
    directive from MASTER; (Pass 3) Appends a studio mix clause from PADDING (30 full
    engineering sentences); (Pass 4) Appends a second non-duplicate PADDING sentence; (Pass 5)

    Inserts micro-clauses from MICROS (15 precision clauses between 24 and 48 chars); (Pass 6)
    Fine-tunes qualifier adjectives to land squarely inside 925–975.
   The 5-Pass Compression Cascade (When > 975 Chars): If length > 975: (Pass 1) Removes
    optional trailing padding sentence if present; (Pass 2) Swaps long multi-clause mix tail for a
    compact MICROS directive; (Pass 3) Condenses secondary sound-design parentheticals; (Pass
    4) Trims third-Clause arrangement qualifiers while preserving all 7 core sentences; (Pass 5)
    Performs clause-boundary truncation at the last semicolon or comma before character 974
    and restores a clean terminal period.


## 8.3 Deterministic 3-Word Title Case Generator (formatTitle)
Every Variant Card is paired with an evocative, non-cliché 3-word Title (e.g., 'Obsidian Garage
Sub', 'Velvet Ritual Pulse', 'Nocturnal Bass Cathedral') generated by formatTitle() in render.ts and
formatted via toTitleCase() in src/lib/suno/title-case.ts. The generator combines a mood/texture
word from TITLES.adj, a sonic/genre noun from TITLES.noun, and an architectural suffix from
TITLES.tail, ensuring zero duplicate words and clean Title Case capitalization.
