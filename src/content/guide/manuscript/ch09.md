# Validation & Repair Rules

## 9.1 The 6 Hard Quality Gates (validatePrompt)
No compiled prompt is ever displayed to the user without passing through validatePrompt() in
src/lib/suno/validate.ts. The validator executes 6 programmatic assertions:

## 9.2 The 14 × 6 Deterministic Retry & Mechanical Repair Loop
In src/lib/suno/generate.ts, generateStylesPrompts() wraps the planner, renderer, and validator
in a two-tier self-healing loop:

   Outer Structural Loop (Up to 14 Attempts): For each of the 3 variant slots, the engine runs
    up to 14 structural attempts (attempt < 14). On each attempt, if a hard gate fails or the variant
    is too similar to an earlier variant in the batch, the PRNG advances to re-sample lexicon
    phrases and Sonic DNA clauses.
   Inner Mechanical Repair Loop (Up to 6 Repairs): Inside each structural attempt, the engine
    runs up to 6 in-place mechanical repairs (repair < 6): stripping any residual negation token,
    injecting missing integer BPM, and re-running the character-window expansion/compression
    cascade until allHardGatesPass() returns true.
