# Rights & Publishing Considerations

## 17.1 Why Artist-Name Stripping Protects Both Prompt Quality and Legal
Chain of Title
Using recording artist names, producer tags, or song titles inside AI music prompts creates two
simultaneous hazards: (1) Suno's server-side content filter blocks generation when it detects
protected artist names, and (2) referencing third-party rightsholders in your generation metadata
clouds commercial chain-of-title when submitting tracks to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube
Content ID, or sync agencies. By stripping protected names in parse.ts (Gate #3) and translating
them into pure acoustic descriptors, Suno Workbench guarantees clean prompt metadata.
