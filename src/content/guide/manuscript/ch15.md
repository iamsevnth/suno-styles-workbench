# Studio Production Workflows

## 15.1 Inside Suno Studio 1.2 / 2.0 (The Agentic Web DAW)
Suno Studio (Getting Started with Suno Studio and Suno Studio Official Announcement)
transforms Suno from a two-clip generator into a full multitrack timeline environment with
agentic stem generation, BPM/time-signature grid locking, warp markers, Audio-to-MIDI
conversion, and the GenFX processing rack.





## 15.2 The 5-Stage Suno Workbench -> Suno Studio 2.0 Master Workflow
Workflow Stage                Step-by-Step Engineering Protocol

Stage 1: Compile & Lock       In Suno Workbench, set an explicit BPM Lock (e.g., 140 BPM) before
Integer BPM                   compiling your 3 variants. Having all 3 variants share an identical integer
                              BPM means clips generated from Variant #1, Variant #2, and Variant #3
                              will align on the exact same bar grid inside Suno Studio without destructive
                              time-stretching.

Stage 2: Multi-Variant        Generate pairs from Variant #1 (Primary Spine) and Variant #3 (Hybrid
Timeline Comping              Contrast) in Suno v6 with Variety = 0. Drop both into Suno Studio on
                              parallel lanes: use Variant #1 for the atmospheric intro, verses, and vocal
                              hook, and comp in the high-contrast bass drop from Variant #3 for Drop 1
                              and Final Drop.

Stage 3: Multitrack Stem      Run 12-track stem separation inside Suno Studio. Mute any stem that feels
Expansion & Overdubs          crowded during the verse, or select an empty 8-bar region on a new lane
                              and prompt a targeted instrumental overdub (e.g., 'melancholic R&B
                              electric guitar counter-melody at 140 BPM') using the exact phrasing from
                              Sentence 4 of your Workbench card.

Stage 4: Audio-to-MIDI &      Use Suno Studio's Audio-to-MIDI export on the Bass and Harmony stems.
Sub-Bass Reinforcement        In Ableton Live, FL Studio, or Logic Pro, layer a pure sine/triangle sub-
                              oscillator (Serum / Phase Plant / Operator) underneath the extracted MIDI
                              notes below 80 Hz while high-passing the AI bass stem at 85 Hz (24 dB/oct
                              Linkwitz-Riley) for rock-solid club sub translation.

Stage 5: Bus Mastering &      Apply gentle glue compression (1.5–2 dB gain reduction, 30 ms attack, auto
Loudness Calibration          release), dynamic mid-side EQ (dipping 250–350 Hz mud in the sides,
                              boosting +1 dB air above 12 kHz), and true-peak limiting targeting -8 to -9
                              LUFS Integrated (-1.0 dBTP ceiling) for club/bass releases or -14 LUFS
                              Integrated for dynamic streaming masters.
