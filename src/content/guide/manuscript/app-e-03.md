Master Library §17. Melodic Rap & Emotional Rap

Core

   Emotional Rap

   Moody Mainstream Melodic Rap

   Future Bass Trap / Emotional Rap

Master Library §19. Gospel Trap
Gospel Trap belongs in the library but remains a minor or occasional hybrid.
Potential contribution:
   Emotional uplift
   Gospel-derived harmonic movement
   Soulful vocal intensity
   Trap rhythm beneath uplifting harmony
It should not be treated as one of the defining genres.


Master Library §20. Bass House
Bass House is another occasional secondary genre.
Contribution:
   Four-on-the-floor energy
   Club momentum
   Bass-forward groove
   House-derived repetition
   Useful contrast against half-time material
Its greatest value is as a crossover device rather than a dominant identity.


Master Library §21. Dirty South Bass
A rare supporting influence.
Potential characteristics:
   Southern Hip-Hop low end
   Heavy 808s


   Slower swagger
   Crunk-adjacent rhythmic energy
   Minimal melodic construction
Useful when a Trap production needs heavier regional Hip-Hop DNA.


Master Library §22. Dancehall & Tropical Dancehall
Rare influences that can contribute:
   Syncopated Caribbean rhythm
   Dance-oriented bounce
   Offbeat rhythmic movement
   Tropical percussion
   Vocal cadence variation
Best treated as rhythmic coloration.


Master Library §23. Reggaeton
Reggaeton is a rare fusion component.
Its primary contribution is:
   Dembow-derived rhythmic movement
   Latin club energy
   Repetitive dance groove
   Percussion-driven momentum
It is not central to the identity but can meaningfully reshape a Trap, UKG, Brazilian, or EDM
hybrid.


Master Library §24. French Filtered House
A rare stylistic departure inspired by late-'90s filtered House aesthetics.
Contribution:
   Filtered loops
   Disco-derived sampling
   Pumping compression
   Repetitive groove
   Warm retro coloration
This sits at the outer edge of the library and is useful when deliberate stylistic contrast is desired.

Master Library §27. Cinematic Horror
Cinematic Horror is best classified as an aesthetic framework rather than a primary dance genre.
Typical vocabulary:
   Ominous drones
   Metallic impacts
   Horror textures
   Dissonant ambience
   Tension risers
   Low orchestral pressure
   Impact hits
   Distorted sound effects
It combines naturally with:
   Ritual Bass
   Dark Trap
   Dubstep
   Cyber Bass
   Industrial percussion


Master Library §28. Dark Occult Ritual Ceremony
Dark Occult Ritual Ceremony is a recurring creative concept rather than a standardized genre.
It functions as an umbrella aesthetic connecting:
Ritual Bass + Dark Ambient + Cinematic Horror + Trap + Dubstep + UKG + Brazilian rhythmic
elements
The emotional objective is:
ceremonial / hypnotic / dangerous / ancient / cinematic / massive / mysterious

   Distorted impacts
   Clangs

Global Percussion

   Ceremonial drums

   Polyrhythmic accents
