# Production Lexicon & Sonic DNA

## 10.1 The 'No Adjective Salad' Producer Rule & Family-Key Gating
As stated in the header of src/lib/suno/lexicon.ts (LEXICON_VERSION = '2026-09-18'): 'Gibberish
rule: every phrase must be something a producer would say in a session. No random adjective
salad.' To prevent genre cross-contamination—such as acoustic folk banjo phrases leaking into a



Cyber Riddim prompt, or industrial techno distortion leaking into an intimate Neo-Soul ballad—
lexicon.ts implements familyKeys(genres).

Before sampling any phrase from the 11 family-keyed banks (PRODUCTION, FEELS, GROOVE,
DRUMS, BASS_TONES, BASS_MOVES, HARMONY, MELODY, SOUND_DESIGN, ARRANGEMENT,
INSTRUMENTS), familyKeys() maps the active genres into one or more of 16 lexicon family keys
('trap', 'future', 'drill', 'phonk', 'bass', 'riddim', 'garage', 'baile', 'ritual', 'cinematic', 'soul', 'house',
'rap', 'dancehall', 'breaks', 'folk', 'gospel', 'pop', plus fallback 'general'). pickFamily() then draws
exclusively from the matching family pools.
