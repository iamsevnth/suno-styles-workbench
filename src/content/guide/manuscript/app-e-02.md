Master Library §7. 140 Bass & UK Bass

Core
   140 Bass Music
   140 UK Bass
   UK Bass
   140 UKG
   140 UKG Bass
   Deep 140 Bass

Specialized

   Deep-Wobble 140
   UKG-Influenced Deep Bass
   Brazilian Funk / 140 Bass
   Trap / Dubstep / 140 UKG Bass

Role
The 140 family is important because it creates a meeting point between:
Dubstep + UKG + Trap + UK Bass culture
It permits:
   Heavy half-time drops
   Garage swing
   Sub-bass pressure
   Sparse rhythmic arrangements
   Wub-heavy bass design
   Dark club atmospheres
This is one of the most natural fusion spaces in the overall identity.

Rhythmic Contribution
UKG introduces:
   Swing
   Shuffle
   Skippy percussion
   Syncopated kick placement
   Rim and snare interplay
   Elastic groove
   Forward rhythmic momentum
This prevents bass-heavy productions from becoming rhythmically static.
A particularly recognizable signature is the contrast between UKG-derived swing in verses or
transitional sections and heavy half-time Trap/Dubstep drops.

Role
Future Bass brings the primary emotional and euphoric counterweight to darker genres.
Typical characteristics:
   Wide supersaw-style harmony
   Emotional chord movement
   Vocal chops
   Bright melodic release
   Sidechain movement
   Expansive drops
   Strong dynamic lift
In this identity, Future Bass is usually most effective when given more sub weight, rhythmic
aggression, darkness, and bass texture than traditional glossy Future Bass.

Character

   Call-and-response bass phrases
   Strong pocket

Master Library §14. Brostep

Character
Brostep represents the more aggressive edge of Dubstep:
   Abrasive growls
   Strong mid-bass
   Dramatic drops


   High-energy bass modulation
   More overt aggression
It is best considered an occasional intensity tool, rather than the default Dubstep personality.
