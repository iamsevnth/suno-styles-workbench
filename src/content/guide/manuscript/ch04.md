# Genre Ontology & Identity Hierarchy

## 4.1 Architecture of the 259-Genre Universe
Suno Workbench's musical intelligence is anchored by a two-layer genre database totaling 259
canonical genres across 21 sound families:

   Layer 1 — The 96-Genre Master Identity Library: Defined in src/lib/suno/ontology.ts
    (GENRES array, 96 entries) and documented in suno-workbench-master-library.md. These 96
    genres represent the core Hybrid Bass Music sonic identity, divided into Tier I (17 genres), Tier
    II (67 genres), and Tier III (12 genres).
   Layer 2 — The 162-Genre Extended Atlas: Defined in src/lib/suno/genre-atlas.ts
    (ATLAS_EXTRA array, 162 entries) plus parser boundary aliases. This layer expands coverage
    across the entire musical spectrum—including Gospel, Soul/R&B, Rock, Metal, Punk, Indie,
    Jazz, Classical/Score, Folk/Country/Americana, Latin, Afrobeats, Reggae/Dancehall, K-Pop/J-
    Pop, Synthwave, Techno, Trance, House, Drum & Bass, IDM, Ambient, and Experimental.

## 4.3 Metric Feel Classification & Tempo Centering
Every GenreDef object in ontology.ts and genre-atlas.ts carries an explicit defaultBpm integer
and one of five rhythmic metricFeel classifications: 'half-time', 'full-time', '2-step', 'four-on-the-
floor', or 'broken'. Why does metricFeel matter so much in Suno v6? Because two genres at 140
BPM can feel twice as fast or half as fast depending on snare placement:
