# Lyrics Studio & Phonetics

## 11.1 Separation of Duties: Styles Box vs. Lyrics Box in Suno v6
A foundational rule of Suno Workbench is strict separation between acoustic conditioning (the
Styles box) and temporal structural/vocal choreography (the Lyrics box). Suno's Styles box sets
the global sonic identity, tempo, instrumentation, and mix engineering across the entire render,
whereas the Lyrics box acts as a real-time timeline conductor—directing section transitions, vocal
delivery shifts, instrumental breaks, and phonetic articulation bar by bar.


## 11.2 Complete Section & Delivery Meta-Tag Taxonomy
The Lyrics Studio workspace provides 1-click insertion buttons for verified square-bracket
structural meta-tags ([Section]) and parenthetical performance cues ((delivery)). Why keep section
tags in square brackets and performance cues in concise parentheses? Because Suno's lyric
parser treats [Bracketed Lines] as structural section boundaries and short (parenthetical cues) as
local vocal/ad-lib modifiers—whereas long, prose-like sentences inside brackets or parentheses
run the risk of being sung aloud as lyrics.

heteronyms (words spelled identically but pronounced differently depending on tense or part of
speech), complex consonant clusters, brand/place names, and raw digits. Both the Lyrics Studio
tab and Skill #3 (Phonetics & Pacing Coach in src/skills/run.ts) include the deterministic American
Phonetics Converter:

The converter supports three selectable intensity modes: (1) Lyric-Safe Mode (replaces only high-
risk heteronyms, numbers, and acronyms while keeping standard spelling intact), (2) Standard
American Phonetic Mode (applies vowel-glide normalization and syllable hyphenation on multi-
syllable words), and (3) Heavy Phonetic Mode (rewrites full vowel clusters into explicit phonetic
syllables for stubborn G2P edge cases or stylized dialect delivery).
